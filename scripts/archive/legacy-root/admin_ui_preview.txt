VISUAL MARKERS:

📄 Full Page (BLUE badge)
   • Has dedicated detail page at /projects/rendering/{slug}
   • Appears in "Selected Works" section on portfolio page
   • Also appears in gallery grid (can open modal OR navigate to page)
   • Examples: Isolation, Northwind Mare Tavern, Ashes, Angel Food Cake

🖼️ Gallery Only (ORANGE badge)
   • No detail page route
   • Only appears in "Archive & Exploration" gallery grid
   • Clicking opens ProcessGalleryModal overlay
   • Examples: All My Sons, Boeing Boeing
